Choose articles before writing to your vault

For a manual workflow, export Markdown and move it into your vault. The full-version integration supports selecting library articles and writing them in batches.

  1. Save the articles you need into the local library. Successful saves or exports retain article snapshots and source information locally.
  2. Use the knowledge-base entry in the library and choose the vault root containing .obsidian, not Downloads or a nested folder.
  3. Filter by source, date or archive collection, select articles and export. Connecting for the first time prompts for a directory even if no articles have been selected.

Your library is not your browsing history

Filters cover only articles you deliberately added or successfully exported. The extension does not scan browsing history or recover articles that were never archived.

Keep images alongside Markdown

MyVault/
├── .obsidian/
├── Example account/
│   ├── An article.md
│   └── _assets/
│       └── stable-article-id/
│           ├── 001.jpg
│           └── 002.png
└── Another account/

Relative image paths let you copy, back up or version the whole vault without relying on machine-specific absolute paths.

Existing notes are protected

  • Identical content can be skipped instead of written again.
  • Different content with the same filename is saved separately.
  • If directory access expires, choose the directory again; permission dialogs do not open automatically in the background.
  • Disconnecting removes the stored directory handle. Reconnect before the next export.

Local-first is not a backup plan

The extension library and Obsidian vault are separate copies. Uninstalling can affect the library; files already written to the vault stay under your control. Back up the vault with system backups, version control, cloud sync or periodic copies.
