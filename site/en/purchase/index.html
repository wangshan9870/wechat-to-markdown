# FAQ

Before you buy

Do store and ZIP versions have the same entitlements?

Yes. The channel affects updates, not free or purchased lifetime entitlements.

Can I keep using free features?

Yes. Ten successful single-article exports are included monthly. Repeating an export or changing format counts separately; a single-article image ZIP counts as one. Failures and cancellations do not count. PDF printing currently does not count; reading, archiving, search and manual JSON backup/restore use no allowance. Existing free users from before the allowance system receive a one-time, non-expiring bonus of 30 exports on their first upgrade; bonus exports are used first.

Does a purchase expire after the deadline?

No. Payment must succeed before October 1, 2026 at 23:59 Beijing time. Paid early-bird entitlements and unlimited exports remain valid regardless of future plans. Lifetime codes sold but not yet redeemed can still be activated after the deadline.

How many devices can I activate?

Up to two devices owned by you. Contact support when replacing a device or reaching the limit.

Are my articles uploaded?

No. Reading, conversion and packaging run locally in your browser; the licensing service does not receive article content.

What if my code is already redeemed?

Use the same code at any activation entry to restore this device. If it fails, keep the complete error and contact support.

Is a manual purchase different from online checkout?

Only delivery differs: online orders deliver automatically, while the maintainer sends manual codes. Price and entitlements are the same before the deadline.
