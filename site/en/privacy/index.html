Scope

This policy applies to the Chrome extension WeChat to Markdown (formerly also named 网页归档助手) and wx2md.com. It is provided by independent developer Wangshan and is not affiliated with, authorized by or endorsed by Tencent or WeChat.

Articles and public collections

When you actively use the extension, it reads WeChat articles, public collections or ordinary article pages you have permission to access. It locally creates Markdown, HTML, PDF, images, ZIP archives and library records. Processed fields can include titles, authors, source names, publication times, bodies, image URLs and source links.

This article content is not uploaded to the operator’s servers. You choose where to save generated files. Library bodies, archive collections, filters and knowledge-base directory handles remain in extension-local storage.

Licensing, orders and necessary requests

When you redeem or activate a paid license, the extension sends a license code or email address, a random installation ID and the extension version to the licensing service to verify entitlements and device counts. That service does not receive article bodies, article URLs, titles, images, Cookies or browsing history.

The website has no license-code input. Enter codes only in the installed extension’s activation screen, available from the toolbar popup, article, collection or local library. A single submission redeems the code and activates the current device.

Online purchases use the operator’s NAS Work service to process products, orders, payment amounts, transaction status and associated license codes. WeChat Pay handles payment. The purchase page sends a product ID and random order-access credential to NAS Work and displays the delivered code after payment. The credential stays in the URL fragment, which is not sent to the static website host; queries send it in the body of an HTTPS request to the order service. Anyone holding the complete order link can view its code. Do not share it. Purchase pages do not load third-party analytics or send order details or license codes to analytics services.

If you add the maintainer on WeChat, WeChat processes your account and conversation data. Share only what is needed for troubleshooting; do not post article bodies, Cookies, Tokens, license codes, payment receipts or identity documents publicly.

External feedback form

The support page links to an external Feishu form. Submitted feedback goes directly into the operator’s Feishu Base; the website does not read, store or transmit form answers. Contact details are optional and needed only if you want a reply.

Feishu may process network, device and account information according to its privacy policy. Do not submit article bodies, Cookies, Tokens, license codes, payment receipts or identity documents in the form or public issues.

Optional product and website analytics

Extension analytics

Product analytics are off by default. You may opt in using the toolbar popup’s privacy and usage controls and withdraw at any time; refusal does not affect export or purchased entitlements. When enabled, allowlisted events go to the operator’s NAS Work service to understand installation, daily use, feature completion and failure categories.

Fields are limited to random installation ID, version, distribution channel, platform, optional interface language, feature entry point, export format, count ranges, broad error category, purchase/activation stage, allowance-rule version, new/existing free-install group, allowance source and monthly usage stage. Event IDs deduplicate retries. These fields measure allowance use and activation conversion; activation is not treated as proof of payment. Third-party analytics credentials remain on trusted servers and are not included in the extension package.

Extension analytics exclude article content, URLs, titles, site names, Cookies, email addresses, license codes and Tokens. Failures do not block export, licensing or the local library. Withdrawal stops future events and clears the unsent queue; previously sent records are not automatically deleted, but you can request deletion privately. The random installation ID links events from one installation and is also used for licensing, so it is pseudonymous rather than irreversibly anonymous. Servers may also record IP addresses and request times; these are not used to analyze article content.

Website analytics

Except on purchase pages, wx2md.com loads Google Analytics 4 only after you explicitly agree. It measures page visits and clicks toward installation, pricing and support. Only sanitized page paths, content categories and click positions are sent. Google Signals and advertising-personalization signals are disabled.

Purchase pages recognize only allowlisted surface and trigger values to show fixed local guidance. Purchase-page visits and conversion sources are not tracked. Unknown values are ignored. Free-text message, URL query strings, form content, article data and full URLs are not read or sent to analytics.

Google analytics are not loaded by default. Decline or withdraw using “Analytics settings” at the bottom of a page. Withdrawal stops future events and removes accessible GA Cookies for this site; previously sent data is not automatically deleted. Request deletion through private support. Your choice is stored in this browser; clearing site data prompts again.

Google processing

Only after consent on non-purchase pages does the website make HTTPS requests to Google. Google may process network and device information under its privacy policy and data-processing terms. Purchase pages do not load Google analytics. We do not use usage analytics for advertising, cross-site profiling, credit assessment or AI training.

Retention and security

  • You store articles in your browser or chosen folders; the operator has no server copy.
  • Free allowances, migration bonus markers and unfinished export records are stored locally to prevent duplicate deductions and recover interrupted operations. They do not contain article bodies. Clear extension or website data to remove local settings and checkpoints.
  • Uninstalling, clearing browser data, changing the official ZIP installation folder or switching browser profiles may make library data and checkpoints unrecoverable.
  • Orders, financial records and licenses are retained as needed for contracts, support and legal obligations.
  • Licensing and analytics use HTTPS. Local SiYuan connections contact your configured local address only when you initiate synchronization.

Your choices and rights

You may stop using or uninstall the extension, clear local browser data, withdraw website analytics, disconnect knowledge-base directories and use private WeChat support to request access, correction or deletion of license contact information and linkable analytics records held by the operator. Transaction records required by law may be retained.

Do not paste article bodies, Cookies, Tokens, license codes, identity documents or other sensitive information into public discussions.
