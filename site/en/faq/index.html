What is WeChat to Markdown?

A Chrome extension maintained by Wangshan, available at wx2md.com. It focuses on reading, saving and exporting WeChat Official Account articles, and also supports ordinary article pages you choose to save.

Are my articles uploaded?

Parsing, conversion and packaging run locally. Article bodies, URLs and titles are not sent to the operator. Image downloads contact their original hosts; licensing and update checks may contact the operator. Optional product analytics are off by default. Read the privacy policy.

Which export formats are supported?

Markdown, HTML, PNG share images and PDF through browser printing. Optional image ZIP archives contain files for offline reading; PDF layout and output are handled by the browser print dialog.

Can I keep using the free plan?

Yes. It currently includes 10 single-article exports per month shared across Markdown, HTML, PNG and image ZIP exports. Failed or cancelled exports do not count; PDF printing currently does not count. See current limits and entitlements.

How do I use Obsidian, SiYuan or Typora?

Export Markdown, then move it into an Obsidian vault, import it into SiYuan or open it in Typora or VS Code. Unzip image archives first and keep the relative folder structure. Advanced integrations belong to the full version. See the Obsidian guide.

Can I archive a full collection?

Yes, through a user-initiated public-collection task. The full version supports complete, incremental, split and resumable archiving. The free collection trial is separate from the monthly single-article allowance: one successful export of up to 5 articles. See the collection guide.

Can it bypass paywalls or access restrictions?

No. Only process content you have the right to access and use. It does not bypass login, paywalls or platform restrictions or grant third-party content rights. Local saving does not grant republication or commercial-use rights. Read the terms.

Does it support ordinary web pages?

It supports article-style blogs, news and tutorials after you actively click the extension. Complex apps, unusual layouts and dynamically loaded content may not be extracted fully. WeChat articles are the primary supported use case.

How does Community source differ from the store version?

The public Community repository contains single-article export, image localization and quick saving. The full store version also contains collection archiving, licensing and paid features. Public source does not cover every store feature. Use the official download page.

Where do I install it? Do I need an account?

Choose the Chrome Web Store or official ZIP on the download page. Basic use requires no account. Follow the first-save guide; paid licensing details are on the pricing page.

Will the local library last forever?

It has no automatic expiry, but it is not a permanent backup. Uninstalling, clearing browser data, changing profiles or moving an unpacked installation can cause data loss. Export or back up regularly.

What if images are missing or export fails?

Check that the original article and images load normally, then retry. Failed image downloads retain their original URLs and are reported; those images may still need a network connection. Visit support for troubleshooting.
